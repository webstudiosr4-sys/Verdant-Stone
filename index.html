About the studio · 02

Specific to the site.
Generous in daily life.

Verdant & Stone is a Richmond landscape design studio working at the intersection of architecture, land, and daily life.

We study topography, vegetation, light, movement, and the way a family lives before shaping one calm, durable framework for the landscape.

How we work · 03

From first walk
to a living garden.

A clear, collaborative process keeps every decision connected to the land and to the way the garden will be lived in.

01

Listen & read the land

We begin on site—learning how the property moves, where light settles, what already thrives, and how you want to live outdoors.

02

Shape the framework

We turn those observations into one clear concept for circulation, gathering, planting, materials, and the relationship to the architecture.

03

Detail & guide

We refine the plan, coordinate the essential details, and stay close through implementation so the original idea remains intact.

OUR APPROACH

From vision to living landscape.

We transform thoughtful plans, natural materials and considered planting into outdoor spaces that grow more beautiful with time.
